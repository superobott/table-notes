Table Notes terms of use

Updated 7 October 2026.

Table Notes is a personal restaurant journal and optional restaurant-discovery app. The journal works offline. Live discovery requires internet and a Google Cloud project configured by the user.

Your entries

You choose the names, grades and comments in your journal and remain responsible for them. Keep exported backups if your data matters to you: local app storage may be removed through uninstalling, clearing storage, or device loss.

Google discovery

Google-provided listings are distinct from your personal grades. Discovery returns a limited selection of nearby restaurants, not a complete directory. Business status and addresses may be outdated or incomplete. Confirm details directly before relying on them. “New to these results” means a place ID newly appeared in this app's search results; it does not mean a restaurant has newly opened.

Daily refreshes are approximate and subject to Android scheduling, battery, connection and app settings. They refresh IDs for your saved area. Live names and addresses load when you view Discover. The app does not scrape the Google Maps application or retain Google content beyond permitted place IDs.

Your Google Cloud project

You are responsible for enabling the required service, protecting and restricting your API key, complying with your Google agreements, and managing charges and quotas. Manual and background API requests may incur charges according to Google's current pricing. No API key is supplied with the app.

Use of Google Maps services is subject to the Google Terms of Service, the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service, and the applicable Google Maps Platform terms for your project. Google's privacy practices are described in its Privacy Policy. Regional terms may differ.
