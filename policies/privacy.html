Table Notes privacy notice

Updated 7 October 2026.

Table Notes is a personal Android restaurant journal. The app has no operator server, registration, advertising, or journal synchronization service.

Your journal

The restaurant names you enter, your grades, comments and optional Google place IDs are stored privately on your phone. The app does not upload grades or comments. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for this app's data. You can export a readable JSON backup to a location you choose; its storage provider then controls that file. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local data.

Optional Google discovery

Discovery uses the Google Places SDK for Android. When you request restaurants, the search area coordinates, radius and requested restaurant type are sent to Google. Google may also process device, connection, application and diagnostic information under its own policies. An approximate device location is read only when you choose “Use my location” and grant permission. Daily discovery uses the saved search area, not background location tracking.

The app retains Google place IDs and update timestamps. Names, addresses and business status from Google are displayed from live responses and are not persisted in the app's database, backups or settings. Your journal's names and notes are entries you author yourself.

Your API key

Your Google Places API key is encrypted using a key in Android Keystore and kept in private app settings. The SDK uses it to authenticate requests to Google. You should restrict the key to the app's Android package and signing identity. The key is not included in exported journal backups. Requests may incur charges in your Google Cloud project.

External links

Opening a restaurant in Google Maps sends the selected name and/or place ID in that link to Google Maps. Opening other links or public policy pages is handled by your browser or another chosen app.

Controls

You can turn off daily discovery, remove Google setup and its saved area, revoke location permission in Android settings, delete journal entries, or uninstall the app. Removing Google setup does not delete your journal.

Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy, which applies to the Google services used for discovery and Maps links.
